Tap & Buy Store — Policies & Legal

Welcome to Tap & Buy Store. Please read our official operational policies carefully before placing your order.

1. Privacy Policy (GDPR Compliance)

We respect your privacy. Any personal information collected (such as name, email, and shipping address) is used strictly for order processing, fulfillment, and customer support. We comply with GDPR standards. Your data is never sold, traded, or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

2. Terms of Sale

Submitting an order form generates a custom price quote and request, which serves as an estimate valid for 48 hours. An order is only officially confirmed and put into fulfillment once full payment is successfully registered and verified through our checkout channels.

3. Shipping Policy

Orders are processed and dispatched within standard fulfillment windows. Standard global delivery takes approximately 10 to 25 business days depending on destination customs and logistics. Fulfillment updates are coordinated through our automated twice-daily processing windows.

4. Returns & Refunds

Return requests must be submitted within 7 days of delivery accompanied by valid photographic or video evidence of transit damage or item defects. Approved refunds are processed back via cryptocurrency equivalent to the original USD purchase value.

5. Cryptocurrency Disclaimer

All digital asset and cryptocurrency transactions are final upon network confirmation. Due to the inherent market volatility of cryptocurrencies, refunds and adjustments are calculated strictly against the original USD fiat value agreed upon at the time of the initial order quote.
